Sonder, KarinationGroup's Korean airline, is flying. Every Sonder flight can
be booked here, like any other flight in the group, and is marked as operated
by Sonder when you choose it.

It flies A350-1000s and 787-8s from Incheon to fifteen cities:

- **North America:** Boston, San Francisco, San Jose and Sacramento
- **Europe:** Paris Orly, Nantes, Geneva, Nice, Marseille, Catania and Malta
- **Central Asia and Africa:** Ashgabat and Khartoum
- **Oceania:** Melbourne and Port Moresby

See every Sonder route on the [route map](/network), or the week's departures
in the [timetable](/timetable?from=ICN&to=SMF).

## K-culture is the product

Korean food, K-drama and K-pop on the screens, and a cabin with a fan's energy
rather than a flag carrier's. Sonder is meant to be the loudest airline the
group has ever had.

## Bags, and the fan bag

Sonder's allowances sit between Karination's and FORZA's, and every cabin adds
a **fan bag** of up to 5 kg for albums, lightsticks and merchandise.

On a journey that also flies Karination or FORZA, the smaller checked
allowance applies throughout, and the fan bag travels on Sonder's own flights.
The full tables are under [baggage](/travel-info#baggage).

## Getting there from Vietnam

Search from your city to any Sonder destination and the connection at Incheon
is built into one booking, with the connection time shown before you choose.

## The name

Sonder is the word for realising that every stranger passing you in the
terminal is living a life as vivid and complicated as your own. For an airline,
that is the whole job.

> Sonder began as a Korean budget airline that stopped flying after the
> pandemic. How it came to us is in [our story](/about/our-story).

Look at the engine of our newest aircraft and it says **30 years of colour**.
That is a young number for an airline that began in 1988, and it deserves an
explanation: Karination has been flying for thirty-eight years, and wearing
colour for thirty. This is how the two numbers fit together.

## 1988 — Before the colour

We began with three Tupolevs and no paint worth mentioning: a white fuselage,
red lettering, and an argument about the name that has still not been settled.
The first revenue flight was VN-A301, a Tu-134, from Sài Gòn to Hà Nội. A
group of engineers and pilots had started an airline because nobody else was
going to, and a livery was the last thing on anyone's mind.

When the embargo lifted in 1994 we waited, and bought used, and bought late.
In 1996 the first Western-built aircraft reached us: a Boeing 767-200, and the
first we received after relations with the United States were normalised.

We did not order a livery. We asked children to design one, and then we let
them paint it. Children painted it. The engineers held the ladders.

The design that won meant two things, as its young authors explained: that
children wanted to celebrate friendship, and that they wanted to live to see
tomorrow's rainbow. It went on the aircraft exactly as drawn.

By 2004 the rainbow had grown up. It was no longer a stripe brushed on a hull
but a ribbon that ran the length of a Boeing 777-200ER, rose through the tail,
and crossed it. Officials from both countries lined up on the apron for the
first flight to Marseille, with French and Vietnamese flags at the foot of the
steps.

It was also the middle of the eighteen months in which we were very nearly
absorbed. The red carpet was our answer: we had somewhere to be, and our own
aircraft to get there.

## 2011 — Colour your dreams

<figure>
<img src="/v2/history/2011-1280.webp" srcset="/v2/history/2011-1280.webp 1280w, /v2/history/2011-2000.webp 2000w" sizes="(min-width: 768px) 42rem, 100vw" width="2752" height="1536" loading="lazy" alt="A white Airbus A320 with purple Karination lettering and teal engines, lifting off past golden rice, a hand-painted COLOUR YOUR DREAMS line along its cabin windows.">
<figcaption>2011: the livery that flew for fifteen years.</figcaption>
</figure>

In 2011 the colour stepped back into the lettering. The aircraft turned white
with a purple name, teal engines, a tail of two sweeping teal strokes, and a
brushed line along the cabin windows that gave the campaign its name:
*Colour your dreams.*

It flew on our aircraft for fifteen years, and the campaign ran for as long.
It was also the year we founded FORZA. For a whole generation of passengers,
that line, in that handwriting, is what Karination looked like.

## 2026 — Made of light

<img src="/v2/hero/three-airplanes-parked-on-tarmac-2400.webp" srcset="/v2/hero/three-airplanes-parked-on-tarmac-1280.webp 1280w, /v2/hero/three-airplanes-parked-on-tarmac-2400.webp 2400w" sizes="(min-width: 768px) 42rem, 100vw" width="2752" height="1536" loading="lazy" alt="Three Karination A321s in the new livery on a moonlit apron: white at the nose, washed in rainbow colour towards a black tail scattered with stars and a sphere of dots.">

This year the line retires. Thank you for fifteen years of it. The colour
goes where it always belonged, onto the aircraft itself: white at the nose,
then flooding back along the fuselage like light through a window, ending in a
black tail with stars on it and a sphere of dots. The lettering is wider than
it has ever been.

Thirty years ago, a group of children asked us to live to see tomorrow's
rainbow. Here it is. We intend to keep going where others do not.

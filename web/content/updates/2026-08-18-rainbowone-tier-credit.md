---
title: RainbowOne qualifying miles now show the night you fly
date: 2026-08-18
category: product
summary: Tier credit used to appear the day after a flight. It now posts when the flight lands.
---

Qualifying miles for **RainbowOne** are now credited as soon as a flight
arrives, rather than in an overnight batch the following day.

If you are close to Amber, Amethyst or Ruby, this matters on the last flight
of a qualifying period: the miles that take you across are in your account
before you have left the airport.

Nothing else about earning changes. Miles are still distance flown multiplied
by your cabin, with your tier bonus applied on top:

| Cabin | Multiplier |
| --- | --- |
| First | 3.0× |
| Business | 2.0× |
| Premium Economy | 1.5× |
| Economy | 1.0× |

Your balance and recent activity are on [your account](/account).

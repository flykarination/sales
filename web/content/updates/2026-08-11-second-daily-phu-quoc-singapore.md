From October, Phú Quốc gains a second daily service to Singapore, giving the
island an evening departure alongside the morning one it has today.

The evening rotation is worked by an A321neo in our two-cabin fit, and is
timed to connect onward the same night at Changi.

- Morning service: unchanged.
- Evening service: daily from October.
- Both are bookable now — search [Phú Quốc to Singapore](/search?trip=oneway&from=PQC&to=SIN).

This is the fourth international route from Phú Quốc and follows the pattern
we have used at Nha Trang: prove the market on a single daily, then add the
bank that makes connections work.

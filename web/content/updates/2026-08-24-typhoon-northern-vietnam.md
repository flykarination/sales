A tropical storm is tracking across the Gulf of Tonkin and is expected to
bring sustained crosswinds to airports in the north through the weekend.

## What this means for your flight

Nội Bài, Cát Bi and Vân Đồn remain open, but arrival rates are reduced while
the system passes. Expect delays of one to three hours on northern sectors,
and occasional consolidation of two departures into one.

Southern and central operations — Sài Gòn, Đà Nẵng, Nha Trang, Phú Quốc —
are not affected.

## If you are booked

- **Change your date free of charge.** Any booking touching HAN, HPH or VDO
  between 24 and 31 August can move once to any date within fourteen days,
  in the same cabin, with no change fee and no fare difference.
- **Cancel for a full refund** if the new dates do not work.
- Do both from [Manage booking](/manage) with your reference and email.

Check [flight status](/flight-status) before leaving for the airport. We
update it from the live schedule, not from a forecast.
